Getting started

  1. Add a show

    Tap + or press ⌘N on Mac. Search by title, choose a result, and select Add.

  2. Check the next release

    Read the status badge beside the show. Select a row to see its IMDb rating and streaming service when available.

  3. Sync or invite

    Open Shows → Settings on Mac or tap the gear on iPhone or iPad. Turn on iCloud sync for this device. Select a personal library and choose Invite to share it privately.

Common questions

What do the status badges mean?

Airing means a season is underway with episodes still scheduled, or a release happens today. A date or release window appears when one is known. Date TBA means there is no known upcoming date.

Ended means the series is over and how it ended is unconfirmed. Concluded means the series wrapped up. Cancelled means the series was cancelled or its story was cut short.

Why is a show unavailable in my country?

Release dates follow the original broadcast or streaming schedule. Check the streaming service for availability in your country.

How do I remove a show?

On Mac, select the show and press Delete, or use its right-click menu. On iPhone or iPad, swipe left on the row and tap Delete.

Does Shows work without iCloud or internet access?

You can use Shows with iCloud off. Saved libraries remain available offline. Searching for new shows, refreshing release data, and iCloud sync require an internet connection.

What if my library isn’t syncing?

Check that you’re signed into iCloud and that sync is on in Shows Settings on each device. Check Last synced, then select Sync Now. To access another person’s library, accept their invitation on a device with Shows installed.
