Privacy Policy

Shows saves your watchlist on your device. iCloud sync and sharing are optional.

Effective November 25, 2026

On your device

Shows stores your library names, saved shows, and cached TV data on your device. The app requires no Shows account and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

iCloud and sharing

If you enable sync, Shows stores library names, saved show entries, and lookup data in Apple’s CloudKit using your Apple Account. When you share a library, invited participants can access its contents, including future additions. Apple handles iCloud and invitations under its privacy policy. Apple Privacy Policy

TV data lookups

Shows sends your show search text to TVmaze and uses show identifiers to fetch schedules from TVmaze, end statuses from Trakt, and IMDb ratings from OMDb. These services may receive standard connection data such as your IP address. You can open IMDb or streaming sites from the app, where their own privacy policies apply.

Managing your library data

Use Shows Settings to delete a personal library. If the library is synced, Shows queues its iCloud deletion. Turning off sync pauses uploads from that device. Local copies and existing iCloud data remain available.

Support and website

If you email support, your address and any information you include are used to handle your request. This site has no forms or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and handles requests under GitHub’s privacy statement. GitHub Privacy Statement

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@bluepeakmobile.com. Policy updates will appear here with a new effective date.
